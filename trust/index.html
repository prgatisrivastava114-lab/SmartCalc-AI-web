What is My Financial Plan?

My Financial Plan (MFP) is India-focused financial-planning software for families and financial partners. It helps people organise goals, understand protection gaps and turn assumptions into an actionable plan. It is not itself a bank, insurer, broker or SEBI-registered investment adviser.

Short answer for families: use MFP for education and planning tools, guided family goal planning and bilingual reports. For regulated investment, insurance, tax or legal decisions, verify and engage the appropriately qualified and registered professional yourself.

Regulatory boundary

MFP provides software-based analysis, financial education and planning tools. The platform does not claim to be a SEBI-registered investment adviser, insurance company or broker. Calculations are estimates based on user-entered assumptions; returns are not guaranteed. Product distribution, investment advice, insurance solicitation, credit services, tax work and legal work must be handled by appropriately qualified or registered parties where required.

Users should ask any professional how they are paid, what registration or credential applies, what service is included, who holds the money or assets, and how complaints are handled.

👴 How Much Retirement Corpus Do You Need — 25×, 30× or 33×?

👴 रिटायरमेंट के लिए कितना कोष चाहिए — 25×, 30× या 33×?

6 min read · English + हिन्दी · by My Financial Plan

The biggest question in retirement planning: “How much money will I actually need?” This guide walks through three famous rules — 25×, 30× and 33× — and explains which one suits India better. Every number is in a table, with all assumptions disclosed.

The rule itself is simple: take your annual expense in retirement, multiply it by the factor — that is the total corpus you need to build.

A quick example: you are 35 today, spend ₹50,000 a month (₹6 lakh a year), and plan to retire at 60 — that is 25 years away. At 6% inflation, your spending by then will be ≈ ₹25.7 lakh a year. Corpus needed: 25× = ₹6.4 crore, or 30× = ₹7.7 crore. The SIP required is calculated below.
Our view: for India, 30× is the wiser target when you consider longer life expectancy, family responsibilities and fast-rising healthcare costs — treat 25× as the bare minimum; the final call depends on your situation.

Three rules, one table

The “25× rule” became famous from the Trinity Study (1998) in the US — the “4% rule”. Its Indian cousins, 30× and 33×, build in a bigger safety buffer:

RuleWhere it comes fromSpend ₹3L/yrSpend ₹6L/yrSpend ₹12L/yr
25×4% withdrawal (Trinity Study)₹75 lakh₹1.5 crore₹3 crore
30×~3.3% withdrawal (safer)₹90 lakh₹1.8 crore₹3.6 crore
33×~3% withdrawal (very safe)₹1 crore₹2 crore₹4 crore

Remember: the “annual expense” here is your expense in the FIRST year of retirement — not today’s.

The inflation punch — today’s ₹50k becomes what tomorrow?

Assuming 6% annual inflation, the size of money grows like this:

Years from nowMoney becomesYour ₹6L/yr becomes
15 years2.40×≈ ₹14.4 lakh
20 years3.21×≈ ₹19.2 lakh
25 years4.29×≈ ₹25.7 lakh
30 years5.74×≈ ₹34.5 lakh

That is why the corpus must be calculated on your future expense, not today’s — otherwise the plan comes up 30–40% short.

How much SIP builds that corpus?

Assumption: 12% a year from equity mutual funds over the long term (this is not a guarantee, only a planning assumption — test your plan at 10–11% too):

Start early = smaller SIP. Starting at 35 instead of 25 makes the SIP roughly 2–3× bigger. And if you can, add a 10% step-up every year — the starting amount becomes smaller still.
EPF, NPS, PPF — they all count towards this same corpus. They look separate, but one team is playing: your retirement.

यही lesson हिन्दी में 🇮🇳

रिटायरमेंट कोष का नियम सरल है — सालाना खर्च × 25 से 33। 25× नियम अमेरिका की Trinity Study (4% नियम) से आया; भारत में सामाजिक सुरक्षा कम और पारिवारिक ज़िम्मेदारियाँ ज़्यादा हैं — इसलिए 30× ज़्यादा समझदारी का लक्ष्य है।

खर्च ₹6 लाख/साल → 30× = ₹1.8 करोड़। और 6% महंगाई से 25 साल में वही खर्च ≈ ₹25.7 लाख/साल — इसलिए आज के नहीं, भविष्य के खर्च पर हिसाब बनाइए।

₹1 करोड़ के लिए SIP (12% अनुमान पर) सिर्फ़ ₹5,322/माह है, अगर 25 साल बचे हों। 10 साल देर कर देंगे तो यही SIP लगभग 3 गुना हो जाती है।

EPF, NPS, PPF, म्यूचुअल फ़ंड — सब मिलकर एक ही कोष बनाते हैं। अलग-अलग दिखते हैं, टीम एक ही है।
आज का छोटा क़दम, कल की आज़ादी — अपना नंबर निकालकर SIP शुरू कीजिए।

Frequently asked questions

Does the 30× include EPF, NPS and PPF?

Yes — the TOTAL of all your retirement assets should reach 30×: EPF, NPS, PPF and mutual funds together. And if you will receive a pension or rental income, the requirement reduces accordingly.

Is the 12% return guaranteed?

Not at all. It is only a planning assumption for long-term equity. Also test the calculator at 10–11% — a good plan always keeps some margin.

Are medical expenses covered in this multiplier?

Not within regular spending. Given how fast healthcare costs rise, keeping separate health insurance and a dedicated medical buffer is the safer approach.

How many years of retirement should I plan for?

Plan from age 60 to 85–90 — that is 25–30 years. A long life is a blessing, but your planning must account for “longevity risk” too.

💹 SIP kya hai — chai se sasta

SIP क्या है — चाय से सस्ता

3 min read · English + हिन्दी · by My Financial Plan

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) means investing a fixed amount every month in a mutual fund — automatically. Think of it like a recurring deposit, but your money buys tiny pieces of businesses, so it can grow faster than FD over long periods.

₹500/month at 12% for 20 years ≈ ₹4.9 lakh. You put in only ₹1.2 lakh — the remaining ₹3.7 lakh is pure growth. That is less than ₹17 a day!

Markets go up and down, but SIP buys more units when prices fall and fewer when they rise — this is rupee-cost averaging. You never have to guess the "right time".

Start with an amount you will never miss — even ₹100–500. Increase it every year by 10% (step-up). Open the SIP Calculator in the app and see your own numbers.
Time in the market beats timing the market. Start small, start today.

यही lesson हिन्दी में 🇮🇳

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) ka matlab hai — mutual fund me har mahine ek fixed paisa apne-aap lagna. Isko recurring deposit jaisa samjhiye, bas aapka paisa companies ke chhote hisse kharidta hai, isliye lambe samay me FD se zyada badh sakta hai.

₹500/माह, 12% पर 20 साल ≈ ₹4.9 लाख। Aapne lagaye sirf ₹1.2 लाख — baaki ₹3.7 लाख poori growth hai। Matlab din ke sirf ₹17 se kam!

Bazaar upar-neeche hota rehta hai, par SIP sasta hone par zyada units kharidta hai aur mehnga hone par kam — isko rupee-cost averaging kehte hain। Aapko kabhi "sahi time" ka guess nahi karna padta।

Utni rakam se shuru kijiye jiski aapko kami kabhi na lage — chahe ₹100–500 hi kyun na ho। Har saal ise 10% badhaiye (step-up)। App ke SIP Calculator me apne numbers khud dekhiye।
Market me kitna TIME rahe — ye TIMEING se zyada mayne rakhta hai। Chhota shuru kijiye, aaj hi shuru kijiye।

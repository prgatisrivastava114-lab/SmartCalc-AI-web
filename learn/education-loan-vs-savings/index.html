🎓 Education loan ya apni savings?

एजुकेशन लोन या अपनी बचत — किससे भरें फीस

5 min read · English + हिन्दी · by My Financial Plan

Parents who have saved diligently often assume the answer is obvious: use the savings, avoid the debt. Parents who have not saved assume the opposite is forced on them. Both are starting from feeling rather than arithmetic, and both sometimes end up worse off.

Here is what actually separates the two choices.

What each option really costs

Paying from savingsBorrowing
Visible costNoneInterest over the loan term
Hidden costEverything that money would have earned, and the buffer you no longer haveA fixed obligation that must be paid whatever else happens
Who carries itYouOften the student, which changes the family’s exposure
FlexibilityMoney once spent cannot be recalledCan usually be prepaid if circumstances improve

The column most people never fill in is the hidden cost of using savings. Money withdrawn stops compounding, and if it was your emergency buffer, you have swapped a known debt for an unknown one — because the next unexpected expense now has nowhere to come from.

The four questions that decide it

1. Is the money you would spend your emergency fund?

If yes, stop. Funding education by removing the buffer that protects the whole household is the one version of this decision that is reliably wrong. A medical event or job loss during the course would then force borrowing anyway, usually on far worse terms.

2. What does the loan actually cost, after tax relief?

Interest paid on an education loan attracts a deduction under the relevant provision of the Income-tax Act, which lowers the effective cost for a taxpayer. How much it lowers it depends on your slab, and the deduction is available for a limited number of years. A chartered accountant can tell you the figure for your situation; a calculator cannot, because it does not know your other income.

3. Would the savings have been working harder than the loan costs?

This is the comparison people skip. If invested money is reasonably expected to earn less than the loan's effective rate, paying from savings is the cheaper route. If more, borrowing can leave the family better off — provided the repayments are genuinely affordable.

Be careful with this argument. The expected return on investments is uncertain; the loan interest is contractual. Treating an assumed return as though it were guaranteed is how people talk themselves into debt they later regret.

4. Who should carry the obligation?

A loan in the student's name, repaid from their own earnings, shifts the cost to the person receiving the benefit and leaves the parents' retirement intact. That is worth something real. Retirement cannot be borrowed for; education can.

The sentence worth remembering: there are loans for degrees. There are no loans for retirement. A parent who empties a retirement corpus for fees has solved a four-year problem by creating a thirty-year one.

If the course is abroad

One structural point is worth knowing: where education is funded by a loan from a specified financial institution, the TCS on remitting money out of India for it is nil, with no threshold, whereas self-funded education remittances are charged above the annual limit. That is a cash-flow difference rather than a change in total cost, since the collection is adjustable against your tax, but it is real.

A sensible default

For most families the answer is not one or the other. Keep the emergency fund untouched, use part of the earmarked savings, and borrow the remainder — ideally in the student's name. That spreads the cost, preserves the buffer, keeps retirement on track, and leaves the option of prepaying later.

यही बात हिन्दी में 🇮🇳

जिन माता-पिता ने लगातार बचत की है, वे अक्सर मान लेते हैं कि जवाब साफ़ है — बचत लगाओ, क़र्ज़ से बचो। जिन्होंने नहीं बचाया, वे मान लेते हैं कि क़र्ज़ के अलावा चारा नहीं। दोनों भावना से शुरू कर रहे हैं, गणित से नहीं।

बचत से देने की छिपी लागत वह सब है जो वह पैसा आगे कमाता — और अगर वह आपका आपातकालीन कोष था, तो आपने एक पता हुआ क़र्ज़ हटाकर एक अनजान क़र्ज़ मोल ले लिया, क्योंकि अगला अचानक खर्च अब कहीं से नहीं आएगा।

चार सवाल जो फ़ैसला तय करते हैं:

1. जो पैसा लगा रहे हैं, कहीं वह आपातकालीन कोष तो नहीं? अगर हाँ, तो रुक जाइए। पूरे परिवार की सुरक्षा हटाकर फीस भरना इस फ़ैसले का वह रूप है जो लगभग हमेशा ग़लत साबित होता है।

2. टैक्स छूट के बाद लोन असल में कितने का पड़ता है? एजुकेशन लोन के ब्याज पर आयकर अधिनियम के तहत कटौती मिलती है, जिससे करदाता के लिए असली लागत घट जाती है। कितनी घटेगी, यह आपके स्लैब पर निर्भर है — यह आँकड़ा कोई चार्टर्ड अकाउंटेंट ही बता सकता है।

3. क्या वह बचत लोन की लागत से ज़्यादा कमा रही थी? अगर कम, तो बचत से देना सस्ता। अगर ज़्यादा, तो क़र्ज़ फ़ायदेमंद हो सकता है — बशर्ते किश्त वाक़ई सँभल सके। सावधानी: निवेश का रिटर्न अनिश्चित है, लोन का ब्याज पक्का। अनुमानित रिटर्न को पक्का मान लेना ही वह रास्ता है जिससे लोग ऐसा क़र्ज़ ले बैठते हैं जिसका बाद में पछतावा होता है।

4. बोझ किसके नाम हो? छात्र के नाम का लोन, जो वह अपनी कमाई से चुकाए, लागत उस व्यक्ति पर डालता है जिसे फ़ायदा मिल रहा है — और माता-पिता की रिटायरमेंट बची रहती है।

याद रखने लायक बात: डिग्री के लिए क़र्ज़ मिलता है। रिटायरमेंट के लिए नहीं मिलता। फीस के लिए रिटायरमेंट का पैसा ख़त्म करने वाले माता-पिता चार साल की समस्या हल करके तीस साल की समस्या बना लेते हैं।

व्यावहारिक रास्ता: आपातकालीन कोष को हाथ मत लगाइए, तय की हुई बचत का कुछ हिस्सा लगाइए, और बाक़ी क़र्ज़ लीजिए — हो सके तो छात्र के नाम।

यह शिक्षा के लिए है, सलाह नहीं। टैक्स कटौती आपकी स्थिति और बदलते प्रावधानों पर निर्भर है — किसी योग्य पेशेवर से पुष्टि कीजिए।

Frequently asked questions

Is it better to pay for education from savings or take a loan?

It depends on four things: whether the money is your emergency fund, what the loan costs after tax relief, what the savings would otherwise have earned, and who should carry the obligation. For many families a mix works best — keep the buffer intact, use part of the earmarked savings, borrow the rest, ideally in the student’s name.

Should I use my retirement savings to pay for my child’s education?

Generally no. There are loans available for degrees and none available for retirement, so spending a retirement corpus on fees solves a four-year problem by creating a thirty-year one. A loan the student repays from their own earnings shifts the cost to the person receiving the benefit.

Is education loan interest tax-deductible?

Interest paid on an education loan attracts a deduction under the relevant provision of the Income-tax Act, which lowers the effective cost for a taxpayer. How much depends on your slab, and the deduction runs for a limited number of years. Confirm the current position and your own figure with a qualified professional.

Does a loan help with TCS on money sent abroad for fees?

Where education is funded by a loan from a specified financial institution, TCS on the remittance is nil with no threshold, whereas self-funded education remittances are charged above the annual limit. Since TCS is adjustable against your tax, the real effect is on cash flow and timing rather than on total cost.

Can I prepay an education loan early?

Usually yes, and doing so early removes more interest than the same payment made near the end, because the early instalments are mostly interest. Check the specific terms of the loan before assuming it, and do not prepay by emptying the emergency fund.

आम सवाल — सीधे जवाब

Term insurance में पैसा वापस मिलता है?

Nahi — pure term plan me poore term tak zinda rehne par kuch nahi milta, isliye premium bahut kam hota hai. Ise "barbaad paisa" na samjhiye: yeh un logon ki suraksha ka daam hai jo aap par nirbhar hain.

Term cover कितना लेना चाहिए?

Aam taur par saalana income ka 10 se 15 guna. Isse behtar tarika HLV method hai, jisme aapke loans, bachchon ki padhai aur ghar ke kharch ko joda jata hai. Human Life Value calculator se apna anumanit figure nikal lijiye.

Term और endowment या ULIP में क्या फर्क है?

Term sirf suraksha deta hai. Endowment aur ULIP me suraksha ke saath investment juda hota hai, par aksar dono kaam average hote hain. Zyadatar logon ke liye behtar rasta — sasta term plan, aur uske saath alag se SIP.

Yeh jawab aam taur par poochhe jaane wale sawaalon par hain, aur educational hain — aapke personal tax ya investment ki advice nahi.

⚡ Prepayment = dugna fayda

Prepayment = दुगना फ़ायदा

2 min read · English + हिन्दी · by My Financial Plan

Prepaying means paying extra towards your loan principal, over and above the EMI. In early years this attacks the loan when interest is at its peak — every extra rupee saves several rupees of future interest.

On the same ₹25L/20yr loan, one single prepayment of ₹1 lakh in year 3 can save roughly ₹3–4 lakh interest and cut the tenure by ~1.5 years. Bonus laga? Loan par daal do.
Choose "reduce tenure" (not "reduce EMI") while prepaying — that is where the big savings hide. Check your loan for prepayment charges; most floating home loans have none.
Prepay early and prepay often — even small amounts. Time is the interest's best friend; make it yours.

यही lesson हिन्दी में 🇮🇳

Prepayment ka matlab hai EMI ke alawa loan ke principal me extra paisa dalna। Shuruati saalon me ye tab attack karta hai jab interest peak par hota hai — har extra rupaya bhavishya ke kai rupaye interest bachata hai।

Usi ₹25L/20 साल के loan par 3re saal ek hi baar ₹1 लाख prepay karne par lagbhag ₹3–4 लाख interest bach sakta hai aur avadhi ~1.5 saal ghat sakti hai। Bonus aaya? Loan me laga do।
Prepay karte waqt "tenure kam karo" chuno (EMI kam karne wala nahi) — badi bachat wahin chhipi hai। Apne loan ke prepayment charges zaroor dekho; zyadatar floating home loans me koi charge nahi hota।
Jaldi prepay karo aur baar-baar — chhoti rakam bhi chalegi। Samay interest ka sabse bada dost hai; ise apna dost banao।

🏥 Health insurance — kitna cover kaafi hai?

स्वास्थ्य बीमा — कितना कवर काफ़ी है

5 min read · English + हिन्दी · by My Financial Plan

Most people choose a cover amount the same way: a round number that sounds reassuring. Five lakh, ten lakh. The number is rarely connected to anything real, which is why it so often turns out to be wrong at the moment it matters.

Here is a method instead of a guess.

Base the amount on what treatment costs where you live

Start from the cost of a serious hospitalisation in your city, at the kind of hospital you would actually use. That differs enormously between a metro and a smaller town, and between a corporate chain and a local hospital. Ask, or look at published package rates; do not assume.

A practical starting point many planners use: cover enough for a significant surgery plus an intensive-care stay at a hospital you would genuinely be admitted to — not the cheapest one available. The test is whether a bad week would require you to sell something.

Then add for anything you already know about: a family history that makes certain conditions more likely, dependants with ongoing needs, or parents in the household whose risk is naturally higher.

Employer cover is worth having and is not a plan

It ends when the job does. That is usually the moment you least want to be shopping for insurance — and you are a year or more older than when you last could have bought cheaply, possibly with a condition recorded in between.

Employer cover is also sized for the employer's budget, not your family's exposure. Treat it as a useful supplement that reduces what you claim from your own policy, never as the policy itself.

The terms that decide whether a claim actually pays

A policy's cover amount is the number everyone compares. These are the clauses that determine what you receive:

  • Waiting periods — most policies will not pay for pre-existing conditions for an initial period, and specific procedures often have their own waiting period. Buying before you need it is the only way to serve these out.
  • Room-rent limits — if your policy caps the daily room charge and you take a dearer room, many insurers scale down the entire claim proportionately, not just the room charge. This surprises people more than any other clause.
  • Co-payment — a share of every claim you pay yourself. Common on policies for older members, and it reduces the premium precisely because it reduces the payout.
  • Sub-limits — caps on particular treatments regardless of your total cover.
  • Network hospitals — cashless treatment usually requires an empanelled hospital. Check that the hospitals near you are on the list before you need them.
Why the room-rent clause matters so much: a policy capping the room at a given daily rate, where the hospital charges more, can see the insurer reduce associated charges in the same proportion. A claim that looked fully covered can settle well short, and the shortfall is not the room difference alone.

Topping up rather than buying one large policy

A top-up or super top-up policy pays only above a threshold you set. Because it never pays the first portion, it costs considerably less than extending a base policy by the same amount. For families wanting a high total cover, a moderate base plus a top-up is often substantially cheaper than one large policy.

The catch is that the threshold must be genuinely covered by your base policy or your own savings, otherwise there is a gap in the middle where nothing pays.

Buy before you need it

Health insurance has a feature that makes delay unusually expensive: it is priced on your age and your current health. Every year you wait raises the premium, and any condition diagnosed in the meantime may be excluded or loaded. Unlike most purchases, waiting for a better moment makes the thing strictly worse.

यही बात हिन्दी में 🇮🇳

ज़्यादातर लोग कवर की रकम इस तरह चुनते हैं — कोई गोल-सा आँकड़ा जो सुनने में भरोसेमंद लगे। पाँच लाख, दस लाख। वह आँकड़ा आमतौर पर किसी असली चीज़ से जुड़ा नहीं होता, और इसीलिए ठीक उसी वक़्त कम पड़ जाता है जब ज़रूरत होती है।

तरीक़ा यह है: शुरुआत इससे कीजिए कि आपके शहर में, जिस अस्पताल में आप सच में भर्ती होंगे, गंभीर इलाज पर कितना खर्च आता है। महानगर और छोटे शहर में यह बहुत अलग होता है। अंदाज़ा मत लगाइए — पूछिए।

कंपनी का कवर काम का है, पूरी योजना नहीं। नौकरी गई तो वह भी गया — और ठीक उसी वक़्त आप नया बीमा ढूँढ रहे होंगे, एक साल ज़्यादा उम्र के साथ, शायद बीच में दर्ज हो चुकी किसी बीमारी के साथ।

जो शर्तें तय करती हैं कि क्लेम मिलेगा या नहीं:

  • प्रतीक्षा अवधि — पहले से मौजूद बीमारियों और कुछ ख़ास इलाजों पर शुरू में भुगतान नहीं होता। ज़रूरत पड़ने से पहले लेना ही इसका इकलौता हल है।
  • रूम-रेंट की सीमा — अगर पॉलिसी में कमरे का दैनिक किराया सीमित है और आप महँगा कमरा लेते हैं, तो कई बीमा कंपनियाँ पूरे क्लेम को उसी अनुपात में घटा देती हैं, सिर्फ़ कमरे का अंतर नहीं। यह बात लोगों को सबसे ज़्यादा चौंकाती है।
  • को-पेमेंट — हर क्लेम का एक हिस्सा आपको ख़ुद देना होता है।
  • सब-लिमिट — कुल कवर चाहे जितना हो, कुछ इलाजों पर अलग सीमा।
  • नेटवर्क अस्पताल — कैशलेस के लिए अस्पताल सूची में होना चाहिए। पहले से देख लीजिए।

टॉप-अप: यह एक तय रकम से ऊपर ही भुगतान करता है, इसलिए सस्ता पड़ता है। बड़ी कुल सुरक्षा चाहिए तो सामान्य बेस पॉलिसी + टॉप-अप अक्सर एक बड़ी पॉलिसी से काफ़ी सस्ता बैठता है। शर्त यह कि बीच की रकम आपकी बेस पॉलिसी या बचत से सच में ढकी हो।

ज़रूरत से पहले ख़रीदिए। प्रीमियम उम्र और मौजूदा सेहत पर तय होता है। हर बीता साल दाम बढ़ाता है, और बीच में पकड़ी गई कोई बीमारी कवर से बाहर हो सकती है।

यह शिक्षा के लिए है, सलाह नहीं। हम बीमा नहीं बेचते, न बीमाकर्ता हैं न ब्रोकर। आपकी पॉलिसी का दस्तावेज़ ही अंतिम है — ख़रीदने से पहले पढ़िए।

Frequently asked questions

How much health cover do I actually need?

Base it on what a serious hospitalisation costs at a hospital you would genuinely use in your own city, rather than on a round number. Add for known family history and for dependants whose risk is higher, such as elderly parents in the household. The practical test is whether a bad week would force you to sell an asset.

Is my employer health policy enough?

It is worth having but it is not a plan, because it ends with the job — typically when you are least able to replace it, a year older, and possibly with a condition recorded since. It is also sized for the employer’s budget rather than your family’s exposure. Treat it as a supplement to your own cover.

What is a room-rent limit and why does it matter so much?

It caps the daily room charge the policy will pay for. If you take a dearer room, many insurers reduce the entire claim proportionately rather than just the room component, so a claim that appeared fully covered can settle well short. It is the clause that surprises claimants most often.

What is a top-up policy?

A policy that pays only above a threshold you choose. Because it never pays the first portion of a claim, it costs considerably less than extending a base policy by the same amount. The threshold must be genuinely covered by your base policy or your own savings, or there is a gap in the middle where nothing pays.

Should I wait until I am older to buy health insurance?

Waiting makes it strictly worse. Premiums are set by age and current health, so every year raises the price, and any condition diagnosed while you wait may be excluded or loaded. Waiting periods for pre-existing conditions also only begin once the policy starts, so buying early is the only way to serve them out before you need to claim.

आम सवाल — सीधे जवाब

Term insurance में पैसा वापस मिलता है?

Nahi — pure term plan me poore term tak zinda rehne par kuch nahi milta, isliye premium bahut kam hota hai. Ise "barbaad paisa" na samjhiye: yeh un logon ki suraksha ka daam hai jo aap par nirbhar hain.

Term cover कितना लेना चाहिए?

Aam taur par saalana income ka 10 se 15 guna. Isse behtar tarika HLV method hai, jisme aapke loans, bachchon ki padhai aur ghar ke kharch ko joda jata hai. Human Life Value calculator se apna anumanit figure nikal lijiye.

Term और endowment या ULIP में क्या फर्क है?

Term sirf suraksha deta hai. Endowment aur ULIP me suraksha ke saath investment juda hota hai, par aksar dono kaam average hote hain. Zyadatar logon ke liye behtar rasta — sasta term plan, aur uske saath alag se SIP.

Yeh jawab aam taur par poochhe jaane wale sawaalon par hain, aur educational hain — aapke personal tax ya investment ki advice nahi.

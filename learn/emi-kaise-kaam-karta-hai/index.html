🏠 EMI ka poora sach

EMI का पूरा सच

3 min read · English + हिन्दी · by My Financial Plan

EMI looks small and friendly, but the real cost of a loan is the INTEREST you pay over the years. In the first few years of a home loan, most of your EMI is interest — very little goes to reducing the loan.

₹25 lakh home loan, 8.5%, 20 years → EMI ≈ ₹21,700/month → total paid ≈ ₹52 lakh. Interest alone = ₹27 lakh — more than the loan itself!

Two knobs change everything: tenure and rate. Cutting 20 years to 15 raises EMI a little (≈₹2,900) but saves ≈ ₹9.4 lakh interest. Even 0.5% lower rate saves lakhs on a big loan.

Rule of thumb: total EMIs ≤ 40% of take-home income. Use the app's EMI Calculator before signing anything — it shows the true total cost, live.
Compare loans by TOTAL COST, not by monthly EMI. Small EMI + long tenure = silently expensive.

यही lesson हिन्दी में 🇮🇳

EMI chhoti aur pyari lagti hai, par loan ka asli daam hai woh INTEREST jo aap saalon tak dete ho। Home loan ke shuru ke saalon me EMI ka zyada tar hissa interest hota hai — loan kam bahut thoda hota hai।

₹25 लाख home loan, 8.5%, 20 साल → EMI ≈ ₹21,700/माह → kul bhugtan ≈ ₹52 लाख। Sirf interest = ₹27 लाख — loan se bhi zyada!

Do cheezein sab badal deti hain: avadhi (tenure) aur rate। 20 se 15 saal karne par EMI thodi badhti hai (≈₹2,900) par ≈ ₹9.4 लाख interest bachta hai। Bade loan par 0.5% kam rate bhi lakhon bachata hai।

Angootha-niyam: saari EMIs milakar take-home ka 40% se kam। Sign karne se pehle app ka EMI Calculator zaroor chalao — woh asli kul laagat live dikhata hai।
Loan ko monthly EMI se nahi, KUL LAAGAT se tulna karo। Chhoti EMI + lambi avadhi = chupke se mehnga।

❄️ Compounding — the snowball secret

Compounding — बर्फ़ के गोले वाला राज़

3 min read · English + हिन्दी · by My Financial Plan

Compounding means your returns start earning their own returns. Like a snowball rolling downhill — small at first, then suddenly huge. The magic ingredient is not money; it is TIME.

₹10,000/month at 12%: in 10 years ≈ ₹23 lakh. In 20 years ≈ ₹99 lakh. In 30 years ≈ ₹3.5 crore! The last 10 years create more wealth than the first 20 combined.

This is why a 25-year-old investing ₹5,000/month can retire richer than a 40-year-old investing ₹15,000/month — the early money gets more years to multiply.

Do not wait for a "big amount". The best day to start was 10 years ago; the second best is today.

यही lesson हिन्दी में 🇮🇳

Compounding ka matlab hai — aapke munafe par bhi munafa lagna। Jaise barf ka gola dheere-dheere badhta chala jata hai। Asli jaadu paisa nahi, SAMAY hai।

₹10,000/माह 12% पर: 10 साल ≈ ₹23 लाख। 20 साल ≈ ₹99 लाख। 30 साल ≈ ₹3.5 करोड़! Aakhri 10 saal, pehle 20 saal se zyada daulat banate hain।

Isi liye 25 saal ka vyakti jo ₹5,000/माह lagata hai, 40 saal ke ₹15,000/माह lagane wale se ameer retire ho sakta hai — jaldi laga paisa zyada saal tak badhta hai।

"Badi rakam" ka intezaar mat kijiye। Shuru karne ka best din 10 saal pehle tha; dusra best din aaj hai।

आम सवाल — सीधे जवाब

Rule of 72 क्या है?

72 ko apne return rate se divide kijiye — utne saal me paisa double hota hai. 12% par 72 ÷ 12 = 6 saal. Yeh shortcut compounding ka asar samajhne ka sabse aasan tarika hai.

Compounding का असली फायदा कब मिलता है?

Aakhri saalon me. 20 saal ke SIP me growth ka bada hissa 15ve saal ke baad banta hai, kyunki tab tak base bahut bada ho chuka hota hai. Isliye do baatein zaroori hain — jaldi shuru karna, aur beech me rok na dena.

Simple और compound interest में क्या फर्क है?

Simple interest sirf mool dhan par lagta hai. Compound interest mool dhan ke saath-saath kamaye hue interest par bhi lagta hai. Shuru me farak chhota lagta hai, lekin lambe samay me yeh farak bahut bada ho jata hai.

Yeh jawab aam taur par poochhe jaane wale sawaalon par hain, aur educational hain — aapke personal tax ya investment ki advice nahi.

👴 Retirement ka seedha hisaab

Retirement का सीधा हिसाब

3 min read · English + हिन्दी · by My Financial Plan

Retirement planning answers one question: after the salary stops, kaun deta hai monthly income? The answer is your CORPUS — a big enough pile whose returns pay your expenses forever.

Quick rule: yearly expense × 25. Expenses ₹40,000/month (₹4.8L/year) → target corpus ≈ ₹1.2 crore in today's money. Sounds scary? Spread over 25–30 years of SIPs it is very reachable.

This app's Family Plan splits retirement into 3 buckets — essential expenses guaranteed, comforts from safe growth, and a legacy bucket. You see the exact monthly SIP needed, after inflation.

Start retirement savings in your 20s–30s, even symbolically. A ₹2,000 SIP at 25 grows to more than a ₹10,000 SIP started at 45.
Retirement is not an age, it is a number — your corpus. The plan tells you the number and the path.

यही lesson हिन्दी में 🇮🇳

Retirement planning ek hi sawaal ka jawab deti hai: salary band hone ke baad monthly income kaun dega? Jawab hai aapka CORPUS — itna bada fund jiske returns se aapka kharch hamesha chale।

Aasaan niyam: saal ka kharch × 25। Kharch ₹40,000/माह (₹4.8L/साल) → target corpus ≈ ₹1.2 करोड़ aaj ke hisaab se। Dar lagta hai? 25–30 saal ki SIP me baanto to bilkul mumkin hai।

Is app ka Family Plan retirement ko 3 buckets me baantta hai — zaroori kharch guaranteed, aaraam wali cheezein safe growth se, aur ek legacy bucket। Inflation ke baad exact monthly SIP aapke saamne hoti hai।

Retirement saving 20–30 की age me hi shuru karo, chahe naam ke paiso se hi। 25 par shuru ₹2,000 SIP, 45 par shuru ₹10,000 SIP se zyada ban jata hai।
Retirement koi umar nahi, ek number hai — aapka corpus। Plan aapko number bhi batata hai aur raasta bhi।

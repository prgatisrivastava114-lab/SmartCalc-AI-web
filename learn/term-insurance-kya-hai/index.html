🛡️ Term insurance — family ki shield

Term insurance — परिवार की ढाल

3 min read · English + हिन्दी · by My Financial Plan

If the earning member is not there tomorrow, who pays the school fees and EMIs? Term insurance answers this: a small yearly premium buys a very large cover (₹1 crore+) that your family receives if anything happens to you.

Term plan = pure protection, no money-back. That is exactly why it is cheap — like paying a watchman to guard your house; you do not expect your salary back from him.

A healthy 30-year-old can get ₹1 crore cover for roughly ₹800–1,000/month — less than one family pizza outing. Waiting till 40 nearly doubles the price.
Cover = 10–15× your annual income. The Family Plan in this app computes your exact shield (HLV) before suggesting any investment.
First insure the earning hands, then invest. Investment without insurance is a house without a foundation.

यही lesson हिन्दी में 🇮🇳

Agar kamane wala kal na raha, to school fees aur EMI kaun bharega? Term insurance isi ka jawab hai: chhota yearly premium, bahut bada cover (₹1 करोड़+) — agar aapko kuch ho jaye to parivaar ko milta hai।

Term plan = sirf suraksha, paisa wapas nahi milta। Yehi wajah hai ki ye sasta hai — jaise ghar ki rakhwali ke liye chowkidar ki salary; usse paisa wapas nahi maangte।

30 saal ka swasth vyakti ₹1 करोड़ का cover लगभग ₹800–1,000/माह me le sakta hai — ek pizza-outing se bhi kam। 40 tak rukne par daam lagbhag dugna।
Cover = aapki saalana income ka 10–15 guna। Is app ka Family Plan investment se PEHLE aapki exact shield (HLV) nikal deta hai।
Pehle kamane wale haath ka insurance, phir investment। Bina insurance ka investment, bina neev ka ghar hai।

आम सवाल — सीधे जवाब

Term insurance में पैसा वापस मिलता है?

Nahi — pure term plan me poore term tak zinda rehne par kuch nahi milta, isliye premium bahut kam hota hai. Ise "barbaad paisa" na samjhiye: yeh un logon ki suraksha ka daam hai jo aap par nirbhar hain.

Term cover कितना लेना चाहिए?

Aam taur par saalana income ka 10 se 15 guna. Isse behtar tarika HLV method hai, jisme aapke loans, bachchon ki padhai aur ghar ke kharch ko joda jata hai. Human Life Value calculator se apna anumanit figure nikal lijiye.

Term और endowment या ULIP में क्या फर्क है?

Term sirf suraksha deta hai. Endowment aur ULIP me suraksha ke saath investment juda hota hai, par aksar dono kaam average hote hain. Zyadatar logon ke liye behtar rasta — sasta term plan, aur uske saath alag se SIP.

Yeh jawab aam taur par poochhe jaane wale sawaalon par hain, aur educational hain — aapke personal tax ya investment ki advice nahi.

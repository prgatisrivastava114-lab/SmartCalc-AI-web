🌂 Emergency fund — pehla kadam

Emergency fund — पहला क़दम

2 min read · English + हिन्दी · by My Financial Plan

Before any investment, keep aside 3–6 months of your household expenses in a safe, instantly available place — savings account or liquid fund. This is your umbrella for job loss, illness or a sudden repair.

Monthly expense ₹40,000 → emergency fund target = ₹1.2–2.4 lakh. Build it slowly: ₹5,000/month fills it in 2–4 years, and then it just sits there protecting every other investment.
Never invest this fund in shares or lock it in long FDs. Boring is beautiful here — the goal is instant availability, not returns.
Emergency fund first, everything else after. One hospital bill should never force you to break your SIP.

यही lesson हिन्दी में 🇮🇳

Koi bhi investment karne se pehle, ghar ke 3–6 mahine ke kharch barabar paisa safe aur turant milne wali jagah rakho — savings account ya liquid fund। Naukri jaana, bimari ya achanak kharch — ye aapki chhaata hai।

Mahine ka kharch ₹40,000 → emergency fund ka target = ₹1.2–2.4 लाख। Dheere-dheere banao: ₹5,000/माह se 2–4 saal me poora — phir ye aapke baaki sab investments ki raksha karta rahega।
Is fund ko kabhi shares me mat lagao aur lambi FD me mat bandho। Yahan "boring" hi sundar hai — maqsad returns nahi, turant milna hai।
Pehle emergency fund, baaki sab baad me। Ek hospital bill aapko SIP todne par majboor nahi karna chahiye।

आम सवाल — सीधे जवाब

Emergency fund कितना होना चाहिए?

Salaried ho to 3 se 6 mahine ka kharcha. Self-employed ya business ho to 6 se 12 mahine, kyunki income aniyamit hoti hai. Ghar ke total mahine kharch ko 6 se guna kar lijiye — yahi aapka minimum number hai.

Emergency fund कहाँ रखें?

Wahan jahan turant nikal sake aur value na gire — savings account, sweep-in FD ya liquid fund. Equity me nahi rakhiye, kyunki market girne aur zaroorat padne ka waqt aksar ek hi hota hai.

Emergency fund और savings में फर्क क्या है?

Savings kisi goal ke liye hoti hai, emergency fund kisi bhi anishchit jhatke ke liye. Dono alag rakhiye — warna ek hi emergency dono khatam kar degi.

Yeh jawab aam taur par poochhe jaane wale sawaalon par hain, aur educational hain — aapke personal tax ya investment ki advice nahi.

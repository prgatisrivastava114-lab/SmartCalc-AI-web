🍰 50/30/20 — salary ka perfect cut

50/30/20 — सैलरी का परफ़ेक्ट कट

2 min read · English + हिन्दी · by My Financial Plan

The simplest budget on earth: take your after-tax salary and cut the cake three ways — 50% for NEEDS (rent, food, school), 30% for WANTS (outings, shopping, OTT), 20% for SAVINGS & investments.

₹60,000 take-home → ₹30,000 needs + ₹18,000 wants + ₹12,000 savings. If needs eat 70%, trim wants a little — never touch the 20% first.
Make the 20% leave the account on salary day itself — autodate your SIP for the 2nd of the month. Willpower is unreliable; automation wins.
Pay yourself first. Spending what is left after saving beats saving what is left after spending.

यही lesson हिन्दी में 🇮🇳

Duniya ka sabse aasaan budget: tax ke baad wali salary ko teen hisso me kaato — 50% ZARURAT ke liye (kiraya, khana, school), 30% SHOUK ke liye (ghoomna, shopping, OTT), 20% BACHAT aur investments ke liye।

₹60,000 take-home → ₹30,000 zaroorat + ₹18,000 shauk + ₹12,000 bachat। Agar zaroorat 70% kha rahi hai, to shauk se thoda kaato — 20% ko sabse aakhir me chhuo।
20% ko salary ke din hi account se nikal do — SIP ki date mahine ki 2 tarikh rakho। Iraade par bharosa kam, automation par zyada — automation hi jeetata hai।
Pehle khud ko payment karo। Bachat ke baad jo bache use kharch karna — kharch ke baad bachane se hamesha behtar hai।

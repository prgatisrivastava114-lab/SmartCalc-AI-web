🎈 Inflation — chupchaap chor

Inflation — चुपचाप चोर

2 min read · English + हिन्दी · by My Financial Plan

₹100 today will not buy what ₹100 buys in 2045. At 6% inflation, prices double roughly every 12 years. Your future goals — college, marriage, retirement — will cost far more than today's price tags.

Class-11 engineering admission costing ₹10 lakh today will need roughly ₹24+ lakh in 15 years at 6% inflation. Planning for ₹10 lakh means falling short by 60%!

This is why "safe" cash under the mattress is actually risky — it silently loses value every single year. Your money must grow at least as fast as inflation, ideally faster.

Always plan goals in tomorrow's prices, not today's. Every goal in this app is auto-adjusted for inflation — that is the honest number.

यही lesson हिन्दी में 🇮🇳

Aaj ka ₹100, 2045 me utna nahi khareed payega। 6% inflation par daam lagbhag har 12 saal me dugna। Aapke future ke goals — college, shaadi, retirement — aaj ke daam se kahin zyada mehngi padengi।

Aaj ₹10 लाख me hone wali engineering padhai, 15 saal baad 6% inflation par lagbhag ₹24+ लाख maangegi। ₹10 लाख ki planning ka matlab 60% se kam pad jaana!

Isi liye gaddi ke neeche "safe" rakha cash asal me risky hai — woh har saal chupke se value kho deta hai। Aapka paisa kam se kam inflation jitna to badhe, behtar ho ki usse zyada।

Goals ki planning hamesha kal ke daam par karo, aaj ke nahi। Is app ka har goal inflation ke liye auto-adjust hota hai — wahi imaandaar number hai।

आम सवाल — सीधे जवाब

6% inflation का मतलब क्या है?

Aaj ka ₹100, 6% inflation par 12 saal baad takriban ₹50 ki value ka reh jata hai. Matlab aapki kharidne ki shakti aadhi ho gayi. Isi liye 10-20 saal ke goals me inflation ka hisaab lagana zaroori hai.

क्या FD inflation को हरा देती है?

Aksar nahi. FD par 7% milta hai, usme aapke slab ka tax lagta hai — 30% slab me net takriban 4.9% bachta hai. Agar inflation 6% hai to asli return negative ho jata hai. Isi liye lambe goals me equity ka hissa zaroori hota hai.

Salary बढ़ने से inflation पूरा हो जाता है?

Sirf tab jab salary badhne ki raftaar inflation se zyada ho. Aksar dono 5-8% ke aas-paas chalte hain, isliye salary badhne par bachat waisi hi rehti hai — jab tak aap investment ka hissa khud na badhaiye.

Yeh jawab aam taur par poochhe jaane wale sawaalon par hain, aur educational hain — aapke personal tax ya investment ki advice nahi.

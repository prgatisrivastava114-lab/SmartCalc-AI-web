🧾 New vs Old tax regime — simple

New vs Old tax regime — आसान भाषा में

3 min read · English + हिन्दी · by My Financial Plan

India offers two income-tax "menus". NEW regime: lower tax rates, but almost no deductions allowed. OLD regime: higher rates, but you can reduce taxable income using 80C (PF/PPF/ELSS), HRA, home-loan interest, insurance, etc.

Rule of thumb for FY 2025-26: if your deductions are small (under ~₹3–4 lakh), the NEW regime usually wins — and up to ₹12 lakh taxable income (₹12.75L salaried) it can mean ZERO tax via rebate.

Amit earns ₹12L, no HRA, only ₹1L PF/80C → NEW regime: effectively ₹0 tax. Old regime would charge ~₹60k+. But with big HRA + home-loan interest, old can still win — always compare both.
Do not buy random "tax-saving" products just to save tax. Save tax as a side-effect of good investments (ELSS, PPF), never the other way round. The app's Tax calculator compares both regimes in seconds.
Regime choice is a calculation, not a habit. Compare once every year — salary changes, rules change.

यही lesson हिन्दी में 🇮🇳

FY 2025-26 ka angootha-niyam: agar aapke deductions chhote hain (~₹3–4 लाख se kam), to NEW regime aksar jeet-ta hai — aur ₹12 लाख tak taxable income (salaried ke liye ₹12.75L) par rebate se tax ZERO ho sakta hai।

Amit ki kamai ₹12L, HRA nahi, sirf ₹1L PF/80C → NEW regime: tax effectively ₹0। Old regime ~₹60k+ leta। Par bada HRA + home-loan interest ho to old bhi jeet sakta hai — dono ka hisaab zaroor dekho।
Sirf tax bachane ke liye random "tax-saving" products mat kharido। Ache investments (ELSS, PPF) ka side-effect ke roop me tax bachao — kabhi ulta nahi। App ka Tax calculator dono regimes ko seconds me tulna karta hai।
Regime ka chunav habit nahi, hisaab hai। Har saal ek baar tulna karo — salary badalti hai, rules badalte hain।

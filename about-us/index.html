Who We Are

My Financial Plan is a financial planning platform designed to help individuals and families in India understand their financial position, organise their financial goals and prepare for their financial future. It is built for people who want to make structured decisions about money, not for people who already speak the language of finance.

Financial planning is not simply about selecting investment products. A plan only means something once you can see the whole picture at the same time:

  • Income and expenses
  • Assets and liabilities
  • Insurance and protection
  • Investments and savings
  • Emergency preparedness
  • Family responsibilities and children's goals
  • Retirement and other long-term objectives

The platform organises those pieces in one place and keeps the approach in the same order every time:

Understand→Plan→Protect→Invest→Review

Our Financial Planning Philosophy

Financial planning should come before product selection.

A financial product is only suitable in relation to something — your objective, your risk profile, your time horizon, your liquidity needs and the rest of your financial situation. Decide the plan first, and the product question becomes much easier to answer.

Understand

Understand the current financial position before changing anything.

Protect

Identify protection and emergency requirements early, not last.

Plan

Define short-, medium- and long-term financial goals.

Invest

Evaluate savings and investment strategies in the context of those goals.

Review

Revisit the plan as circumstances change — income, family, goals.

Why Financial Planning Matters

Money decisions are connected. What you earn sets what you can save; what you owe and what you protect decide how much of that saving survives a bad year; and what is left has to carry your goals and your retirement.

Income→Expenses→Protection→Savings→Investments→Goals→Retirement

Inflation, changing family responsibilities, healthcare costs, education costs, loan commitments and unexpected events all move those future requirements. A plan does not remove that uncertainty — it makes the size of it visible while you still have time to respond. The effect of inflation and the effect of compounding are the two forces that decide most long-term outcomes.

Planning assumptions are not guaranteed outcomes. Every projection on this site is produced from the figures and assumptions you enter. Actual results will differ, and investment returns can fall as well as rise.

Education Before Product Selection

A financial product should not be chosen because it is popular, because a past return looks attractive, or because somebody earns a commission on it. The same product can be sensible for one household and wrong for the one next door.

Before committing money, it is worth being able to answer seven questions about any product:

Objective

Which goal is this money actually for?

Risk

What can go wrong, and how much of it can you absorb?

Time horizon

When will you need the money back?

Liquidity

How quickly can you access it if something happens?

Costs

What are the charges, and what do they cost over the full term?

Tax

How is it treated on the way in, during, and at maturity?

Suitability

Does it fit your situation, not someone else's?

Our free guides in Money School cover these in plain Hindi and English, and the financial planning tools let you test the numbers yourself before any money moves.

Financial Planning for Families in India

My Financial Plan is built for Indian households, and that shapes the defaults rather than just the language. Planning here usually has to account for:

  • Joint and extended family financial structures, and dependants on both sides
  • Inflation, which affects education and healthcare faster than general prices
  • Education costs that arrive on a fixed timetable
  • Retirement without a universal pension safety net
  • Life and health cover, and what the family would face without it
  • Home loans, personal loans and the EMI load they create
  • The old and new tax regimes, and which deductions survive under each
  • Government-backed schemes such as PPF and Sukanya Samriddhi alongside market investments
  • Income that changes shape — business income, variable pay, a second earner starting or stopping

The service operates online across India. We do not maintain branch offices, and we do not publish location pages for cities where we have nothing genuinely local to add.

Frequently Asked Questions

The answers below are also the answers in this page's structured data — they match word for word.

What is My Financial Plan?
My Financial Plan is a financial planning platform designed to help individuals and families in India understand their financial position, organise financial goals and prepare for their financial future.
What does financial planning include?
Financial planning can include income, expenses, assets, liabilities, insurance, investments, emergency funds, children's goals, retirement requirements and other financial objectives.
Is financial planning only for wealthy people?
No. Financial planning can be useful at different stages of life and at different income levels. What matters is understanding where you are today and where you want to go.
Does My Financial Plan only focus on investments?
No. The approach considers the broader financial picture, including protection, liabilities, savings, investments, financial goals and retirement planning.
Why should I create a financial plan?
A financial plan can help you understand your current financial position, prioritise goals, identify potential gaps and make more structured financial decisions.
How often should a financial plan be reviewed?
A financial plan should be reviewed periodically, and whenever there are significant changes in income, expenses, family circumstances, liabilities, investments or goals.
Does My Financial Plan guarantee investment returns?
No. Investment returns are not guaranteed. Projections on this site are based on the assumptions you enter and actual outcomes may differ.
How can I start?
Begin by understanding your current financial position and identifying your important goals. The free planning tools on this site build that picture in a few minutes, with no sign-up required.

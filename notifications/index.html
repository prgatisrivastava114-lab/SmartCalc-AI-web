Checking…

Notifications are off

Turn them on to get a short alert when we publish something useful. About once a month.

We never put your personal or financial details in a notification — no amounts, no client names, no account numbers. Ever.

Your browser is blocking notifications

We can’t ask again — only you can change this.

  1. Tap the lock icon next to the web address
  2. Find Notifications in the list
  3. Switch it to Allow
  4. Reload this page

Everything else on the site works exactly as normal while notifications are blocked.

Add to Home Screen first

On iPhone and iPad, Apple only allows notifications for sites you’ve added to your Home Screen.

  1. Tap Share at the bottom of Safari
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen
  3. Open My Financial Plan from your Home Screen
  4. Come back to this page and switch notifications on

Requires iOS 16.4 or later.

Notifications aren’t available in this browser

This browser doesn’t support web notifications.

Nothing else is affected — every calculator and tool on the site works normally.

This device

Your subscription is stored against a random ID — not your name, email or phone number.

What we store

A notification token from your browser, a random device ID, the categories you chose, your device type and language, and the dates. That’s all. No name, email, phone number or anything from your financial plans. Privacy policy.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 13, 2026 · Entity: My Financial Plan (MyFinancialPlan.in / SmartCalc AI)

At My Financial Plan, your privacy is paramount. This policy details how we manage and safeguard your financial calculations and personal data across our Web and Android applications.

1. Local Storage First (Client-Side Math)

When using our 500+ financial calculators without logging in, all numbers (income, SIPs, loans, age) are calculated 100% locally inside your browser (localStorage). No calculator entries are transmitted to our servers.

2. Google Sign-In & Cloud Sync

When signing in via Google Identity (auth.html), we store basic profile details (email address and name) to authenticate and sync your saved financial plans securely across devices in Google Cloud Firestore (smartcalc-ai-638a9).

3. We Never Sell Your Data

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to third-party telemarketers, credit bureaus, or ad networks.

You have the right to request full account and data deletion at any time by emailing care@myfinancialplan.in or WhatsApping +91 88401 92702.

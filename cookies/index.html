Cookies & Local Storage Policy

Effective Date: September 13, 2026 · Entity: My Financial Plan (MyFinancialPlan.in / SmartCalc AI)

This Cookies & Local Storage Policy explains how My Financial Plan (www.myfinancialplan.in) uses cookies and browser local storage technologies to enhance user experience, preserve your financial calculations, and secure user sessions.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and browser local storage (`localStorage` and `sessionStorage`) are small data files stored on your device when you visit websites. They allow web applications to remember your actions, preferences, and progress over time without requiring you to re-enter information continuously.

2. Types of Storage We Use

We use minimal, essential browser storage technologies categorized as follows:

  • Essential Operational Storage (`localStorage`): Stores your calculator inputs, language preferences (English / Hindi toggle), and saved plan drafts locally on your device. This ensures your financial entries remain intact when refreshing or returning to the page.
  • Temporary Session Security (`sessionStorage`): Used strictly on administrative portals (`/admin/`) to preserve 2FA authentication state during an active browser session. This data is automatically erased when you close your browser tab.
  • Authentication Cookies: Managed securely by Google Identity Services and Firebase Authentication when you sign in, maintaining your secure login state across sessions.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We do NOT use invasive third-party ad-tracking cookies, cross-site profiling cookies, or data-broker trackers. Third-party scripts are limited strictly to:

  • Google Identity Services (`gsi/client`): Required for Google One-Tap and OAuth authentication (`auth.html`).
  • CanvasKit WebAssembly CDN: Required for rendering the Flutter Web application graphics engine (`/app/`).

4. How You Can Control Storage and Cookies

You can manage, block, or clear cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings:

  • Chrome / Edge / Brave: Settings → Privacy and Security → Clear Browsing Data / Cookies and other site data.
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.

Note: Clearing local storage will reset unsaved financial calculator entries and require you to sign in again.

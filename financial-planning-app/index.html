What the app works out for you

  • Financial Health Score — a 0–100 snapshot of where your family stands today.
  • Goal costing — the future cost of education, marriage, a house and retirement, adjusted for inflation.
  • Monthly investment needed — the exact SIP that turns each goal into a funded plan.
  • Retirement corpus — how large your fund needs to be, and what it pays you each month.
  • Insurance gap — how much life cover your family actually needs, based on your income and loans.
  • Downloadable report — the whole plan as a PDF, in English and Hindi.

How it works, step by step

  • 1. Enter your basics — age, income, monthly spending, existing savings and loans.
  • 2. Add your goals — what you want to fund, and roughly when.
  • 3. See the numbers — the app shows each goal’s future cost and the monthly amount it needs.
  • 4. Adjust and compare — change a figure and every goal recalculates instantly, so you can see the trade-offs.
  • 5. Save or act on it — download the report, or take it to a CFP or investment adviser to implement.

What you need before you start

Nothing official — no PAN, no documents, no bank connection. Just rough figures you almost certainly already know. As a guide:

  • Your age and the age at which you would like to stop working
  • Monthly take-home income, and roughly what you spend
  • What you have already saved or invested, and where
  • Any loans outstanding, and the EMI on each
  • The goals you are saving for, and a rough year for each

Questions people ask

Is the financial planning app really free?

Yes. The plan, the health score and the PDF report are free. There is no signup required to run the calculations, and no card details are asked for.

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No. The app never asks for banking credentials, net banking passwords or card details. You type in approximate figures yourself, which is enough for planning purposes.

Does it work on iPhone as well as Android?

Yes — the web app runs in any modern mobile browser, so it works on iPhone, Android and desktop without installing anything. A native Android app is also available to download.

Is my financial information private?

The calculations run from figures you enter and the plan is yours. You are not asked for credentials, and you can build a complete plan without creating an account.

Can I share the plan with my family or an adviser?

Yes. Download the PDF report and share it — it shows every goal, the assumed inflation and return rates, and the monthly investment needed for each one, so anyone can check the working.

About this calculator

Most people ask "what will my SIP become?" Smart planners ask the reverse: "what SIP does my GOAL need?" This free SIP goal calculator answers exactly that — enter target, return and time, and get the precise monthly SIP. This is how SEBI-registered advisors plan every family goal.

Example: ₹1 crore in 15 years at 12%

The annuity maths (same formula as our SIP calculator, solved backwards) gives ≈ ₹19,800 per month. Total invested over 15 years: ~₹36 lakh. The remaining ~₹64 lakh comes from compounding — your money does most of the heavy lifting.

The brutal cost of delay

Wait 5 years and the same ₹1 crore in 10 years needs ≈ ₹42,500/month — more than double. Every year of delay raises the required SIP far faster than income usually grows. The note above the buttons shows your personal delay cost.

Target too high? Three levers

More time (retire 3 years later), step-up SIPs (start small, grow 10%/year — see our Step-Up calculator), or a realistic return assumption. Never fake the return % upward to make the SIP look affordable — the market doesn't take requests.

Frequently asked questions

How much SIP is needed for ₹1 crore?

At 12% for 15 years ≈ ₹19,800/month; for 20 years ≈ ₹10,000/month; for 10 years ≈ ₹42,500/month. Time is the cheapest contributor — start early.

How is the required SIP calculated?

SIP = Target ÷ [((1+i)ⁿ − 1)/i × (1+i)], where i is the monthly return and n the months — the SIP formula solved in reverse.

The SIP shown is too high for me. What now?

Extend the tenure, start with a step-up SIP (smaller today, auto-increasing yearly), or split the goal: guaranteed portion via PPF/SSY + growth via equity SIP.

Should I assume more than 12% to lower the SIP?

No. Plan at 10–12% for equity; if markets deliver more, enjoy the surplus. Planning on fantasy returns is how goals get missed.

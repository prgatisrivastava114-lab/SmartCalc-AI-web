How this calculator works

This calculator works out an indicative LIC premium, bonus and maturity figure for a plan table and sum assured. Here is exactly what it assumes, the equation it runs, and what it leaves out.

STEP 1What does this calculator assume?

Premiums are derived from the plan table and sum assured you enter, at the age you enter, with GST and the shown bonus/FAB rates applied.

STEP 2What formula does it use?

Table-driven, not a single equation: the plan's rate table gives the base premium per ₹1,000 of sum assured, which is scaled to your sum assured, adjusted for age, then GST is added and projected bonuses are accumulated.

STEP 3What does this calculator not include?

This is an indicative figure, not a quotation. The projection does not include the insurer's own underwriting — your health, occupation, medical tests, chosen term and any rider — which decides the premium you are actually offered. Bonus and FAB rates are declared annually, are not guaranteed, and can be lower than the rates used here. The tax treatment of maturity proceeds is not modelled: it depends on the policy's issue date, the premium paid and your income slab. Read the full assumptions list further down this page before relying on any figure.

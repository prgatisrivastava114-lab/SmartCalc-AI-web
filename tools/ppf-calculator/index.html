About this calculator

The Public Provident Fund (PPF) is India's most trusted long-term savings scheme — government-guaranteed returns, zero market risk, and a rare triple tax benefit. This free PPF calculator shows your 15-year (or extended) maturity instantly: no login, no ads.

How does PPF money grow?

You deposit up to ₹1.5 lakh every year; the government declares the interest rate quarterly (7.1% recently). Interest is calculated monthly on the lowest balance between the 5th and month-end, and credited yearly — our estimate assumes you deposit at the start of the year.

The EEE triple tax benefit

Deposit: deductible under Section 80C (up to ₹1.5 lakh). Interest: completely tax-free. Maturity: completely tax-free. No other guaranteed instrument in India matches this — that is why advisors call PPF the backbone of every family's debt allocation.

What after 15 years?

Extend in blocks of 5 years — with or without fresh deposits. The real compounding magic happens in these extension years: a ₹1.5L/year habit kept 25 years crosses ₹1 crore at 7.1%, all tax-free.

Frequently asked questions

Is PPF interest really 100% tax-free?

Yes — PPF enjoys EEE status: your deposit is deductible under Section 80C, the yearly interest is tax-free, and the entire maturity amount is tax-free.

What is the maximum I can deposit in PPF?

₹1.5 lakh per financial year per account (minimum ₹500). Amounts above ₹1.5 lakh earn no interest and get no tax deduction.

Can I withdraw PPF before 15 years?

Partial withdrawals are allowed from the 7th year (limits apply), and loans against PPF from years 3–6. Full closure is allowed only at 15 years, barring rare emergencies.

PPF or SIP — which is better?

PPF gives guaranteed, tax-free but slower growth; equity SIPs give higher long-term growth with market risk. Smart Indian families use both — PPF as the safe floor, SIPs as the growth engine.

About this calculator

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) is the government's flagship scheme for the girl child — one of the highest guaranteed interest rates in India plus full EEE tax benefits. This free SSY calculator projects the exact maturity corpus your daughter receives at year 21.

How SSY works

Deposit anything between ₹250 and ₹1.5 lakh every year for 15 years. The account then keeps compounding at the government-set rate (8.2% recently) until maturity at 21 years from opening. No deposits needed in the last 6 years — the money grows on its own.

Who can open an account?

Parents or legal guardians of a girl child below age 10. One account per daughter, maximum two daughters per family (relaxation for twins/triplets). Open at any post office or authorised bank branch.

Withdrawals and maturity

Up to 50% of the previous year's balance can be withdrawn for the daughter's education after she turns 18. Full withdrawal at 21 years, or on her marriage after 18. Combine SSY (safe floor) with an equity SIP (growth) for a complete child plan.

How this calculator works

This calculator works out the maturity value of a Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana account. Here is exactly what it assumes, the equation it runs, and what it leaves out.

STEP 1What does this calculator assume?

Deposits are made for the first 15 years, each added at the start of the year, and the account compounds annually until it matures after 21 years.

STEP 2What formula does it use?

For years 1–15: balance = (balance + deposit) × (1 + r). For years 16–21: balance = balance × (1 + r).

STEP 3What does this calculator not include?

The rate is an assumption — SSY's rate is also notified quarterly and can change. The scheme's deposit limits (minimum ₹250, maximum ₹1.5 lakh per year), the one-account-per-daughter rule and the partial-withdrawal-for-education conditions are outside this calculation.

Frequently asked questions

Who can open an SSY account?

Parents or legal guardians of a girl child below 10 years. One account per daughter, maximum two per family (twins/triplets get relaxation).

How much can I deposit in SSY?

Between ₹250 and ₹1.5 lakh per financial year, for the first 15 years. The account matures 21 years after opening.

Is SSY completely tax-free?

Yes — EEE status: deposits are deductible under Section 80C, and both interest and maturity are 100% tax-free.

Can I withdraw money before 21 years?

Yes — up to 50% of the previous year-end balance for the daughter's higher education after she turns 18; full amount at 21 years or on her marriage after 18.

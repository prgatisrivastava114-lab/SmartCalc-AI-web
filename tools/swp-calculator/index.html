About this calculator

A Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) is the mirror of a SIP: your invested corpus stays at work while you withdraw a fixed amount every month. For retirees it is the most tax-efficient pension most families never get told about.

How long will my corpus last?

It depends on three numbers: corpus size, monthly withdrawal, and the return the remaining balance keeps earning. If your withdrawal rate stays below your long-term return minus inflation, the corpus can last 30+ years. That is why planners target a 4–5% annual withdrawal rate with 6–7% balanced-fund returns.

SWP vs bank FD for pension?

FD interest is fully taxable every year; SWP withdrawals are partly capital and partly gains — historically far more tax-efficient and usually higher net income.

What if markets crash in my first years?

Sequence-of-returns risk is real: keep 2 years of withdrawals in a liquid/FD bucket, SWP from the rest. Our Plan page builds this bucket automatically.

Frequently asked questions

What is a safe SWP withdrawal rate?

4–5% of corpus per year is the widely used safe rate for a 25–30 year retirement with balanced funds.

Are SWP withdrawals taxable?

Each withdrawal has a gain component taxed as capital gains (equity LTCG above ₹1.25L/yr at 12.5%) — usually far less tax than FD interest slabs.

Can I change the withdrawal amount later?

Yes, SWP amount and date are editable anytime from your fund platform.
